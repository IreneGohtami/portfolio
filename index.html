Blinders.ai

Turns a single YouTube URL into an end-to-end AI content analysis: it scrapes the video, runs machine-learning models to summarise the content and read its sentiment, then forecasts how the video will perform over the next six months across views, likes, comments, and engagement. The result is a quick read on what a video is about and where it is headed.

  • Supabase
